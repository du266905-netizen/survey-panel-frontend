# Acceptable Use Policy

Effective 1 September 2026

This policy sets out what may and may not be done with the platform, who it applies to, and what follows when it is not. For a specific study, the Research Terms control.

## 1. What we support

We support research that rests on evidence: understanding how real people judge, choose and act, in a way that can be checked afterwards. The platform gives researchers questionnaire design, sample and fieldwork, quota control, quality checks and result handling; it gives participants a clear account of what they are joining and a fair return for their time.

We ask the same of the research itself: a legitimate purpose, adequate notice, the minimum data needed, and conclusions that were not fixed in advance. The conduct below is inconsistent with that.

## 2. Content standards

The platform may not be used to carry out, enable or conceal:

- conduct that breaks applicable law, regulation or supervisory requirements;
- fraud, pyramid schemes, unauthorised fundraising, gambling, sexual services or other commercial illegality under the name of research;
- impersonating a government body, public institution, bank, medical provider or another person in order to obtain trust or information;
- recruiting participants through misleading premises, invented identities or fabricated credentials;
- incitement, hatred, harassment, threats or defamation directed at a person or a group;
- attempts to identify participants, trace individuals backwards, or use research data for doxxing;
- using results to manipulate an election, spread disinformation, or pass work off as independent research.

## 3. Information we do not collect

The platform is not built to collect sensitive personal information. Unless we have agreed in writing beforehand and the requirements applicable to that category are met, the following must not be submitted through a questionnaire, a public link or a workspace:

- passwords, verification codes, one-time passcodes or answers to security questions;
- payment card numbers, bank account details or payment credentials;
- government identity documents, passports, driving licences, social security or tax numbers;
- biometric or genetic information, or precise location histories;
- medical diagnoses, treatment records or mental health records;
- personal information about anyone under 18;
- any other information that law treats as a special category requiring separate consent or specific protection.

Where a study genuinely requires such information, contact us first with the purpose, scope, retention period and safeguards. Collection does not begin until that is confirmed in writing.

## 4. Use of the platform and your account

You must not:

- reverse engineer, circumvent or defeat access controls or security mechanisms;
- run vulnerability scans, penetration tests or load tests against the platform, except as part of an agreed security review;
- use crawlers, scripts or automation to access, scrape or copy platform content in bulk;
- circumvent or evade allowance, quota, frequency, seat or plan limits;
- resell, rent or time-share the service, or let unauthorised third parties use it;
- share credentials, or hand a seat to someone who is not authorised;
- register accounts in bulk, misrepresent identity, or automate the collection of credits or rewards;
- use platform content, questionnaires or model output to train a competing model;
- interfere with the platform, or prevent others from using it.

If you find a security issue, tell us through the channel in section 8 rather than testing it yourself or disclosing it publicly.

## 5. AI features

AI features assist design and editing; they do not replace research judgement.

- Output is reviewed by the person using it, who remains responsible for every question, option and line of copy that is published;
- AI features may not be used to produce unlawful, infringing, discriminatory or misleading content;
- AI output may not be presented as a real interview, a real respondent statement or primary data;
- usage allowances may not be avoided through automated calls, batched requests or other workarounds;
- output can be wrong or biased, and material decisions should be verified separately.

## 6. Invitations, incentives and participants

- Invitations go only to people who have agreed to receive them, and state the topic, expected length, incentive and how to withdraw;
- contact lists may not be bought, rented or exchanged, and invitations may not be repeated to people who have not agreed to receive them;
- incentives must be real and redeemable, with no invented reward and no condition that cannot be met;
- participants may withdraw at any time, and submitted answers are handled within the consent they gave;
- an incentive already earned may not be withheld because someone declined to continue.

## 7. Enforcement

We act in proportion to the conduct and its effect, and explain the basis for what we do.

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| First, minor, immediately correctable | Notice, with what to correct and by when |
| Repeated, or affecting participants | Pause the content or collection, and require a fix |
| Unlawful, fraudulent, seriously infringing | Suspend the account or study at once, and terminate where necessary |
| Risk to the platform or other users | Restrict access first, explain afterwards |

A decision may be appealed by replying to the notice. We review and respond within a reasonable period. Where a court, regulator or payment provider makes a lawful demand, we may act first and inform you afterwards to the extent we are permitted.

## 8. Contact and changes

Questions, reports and requests for an exception: support@guanyisearch.com, with “Acceptable Use” in the subject line.

We may update this policy as the service, the law or research practice changes. Material changes are notified before they take effect through the service, by email or by another reasonable method. The updated version applies from its effective date.
