# Privacy Policy

Effective 1 September 2026

This policy explains how guanyisearch uses information from panel participants, account holders and people who contact us about research services, and what you can ask us to do with it.

## 1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy applies to people who register for or use the guanyisearch research panel, use a client workspace, answer a client questionnaire, or contact us about research services. guanyisearch is the data controller for the personal information described here, except where a client-created questionnaire makes that client responsible for the research purpose and use of the responses.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@guanyisearch.com.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect information needed to provide accounts, support participation and research services, administer rewards, communicate with you, and protect the service.

- **Account information.** Email address, display name, password, account role and session information. Passwords are stored in a protected form and are not displayed to our team.
- **Participation and profile information.** What you choose to provide for panel participation: country or region, birth year, gender, education, employment, household information, survey participation, eligibility and completion records.
- **Research contact information.** When you contact us about research services, the name, email address, organisation type, region and the information you include about your research needs.
- **Questionnaire information.** For client-created questionnaires: the questionnaire title, purpose notice, questions, responses, response timing, and limited browser or network-derived information used to protect the response process.
- **Technical and support information.** IP address, browser and device information, approximate location derived from network information, support messages, and what is needed to protect the service and respond to requests.

## 3. Client questionnaires and responses

When you answer a questionnaire created in a client workspace, the client is generally responsible for deciding why responses are collected and how they are used. The questionnaire identifies the client or states that it is a third-party client, and presents a purpose notice before you respond.

We provide the technical service for hosting, securing and delivering the questionnaire. We make the submitted response available to that questionnaire's creator and process it to operate the service, prevent misuse and meet applicable obligations. We do not use a client's response data for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or notice.

## 4. How we use information

- create, authenticate and support accounts and workspaces;
- match panel participants with suitable research opportunities, and administer participation and rewards;
- respond to research enquiries and provide requested research-related services;
- send service-related communications, and respond to support or privacy requests;
- protect the service, detect misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

We process information to provide requested services, meet applicable obligations, protect the integrity of the service and, where required, on the basis of your consent.

## 5. Sharing by category

We do not publish personal information. Where needed for the stated purpose, we share limited information with three categories of recipient:

- **Questionnaire creators.** Responses to a client-created questionnaire are shared with its creator for the stated research purpose.
- **Research partners and service providers.** Information necessary to provide a requested research service, support a research opportunity, or operate service features.
- **Security and communications providers.** Technical or contact information needed to keep accounts and response collection safe, and to send service-related messages.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a legitimate business transfer.

## 6. AI-assisted support

When you use the website support assistant, the messages you send are processed to provide a support response. The assistant draws on published service information and is not a substitute for a person reviewing an individual account, reward, survey or privacy request.

Do not include passwords, verification codes, payment-card details, government identifiers or full financial details in the chat. If you ask to speak to a person, the conversation and the contact details you provide are stored as a private support request.

## 7. Data retention

Account and participation information is generally retained for the life of your account. Research-contact and workspace information is retained as long as needed to provide the requested services and keep appropriate records. Client questionnaire responses are retained according to the applicable client relationship, the questionnaire settings and applicable requirements.

We may retain limited information for longer where necessary for legal obligations, resolving disputes, protecting the service or maintaining reward records.

## 8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, withdrawal of consent, and a portable copy of the information you provided to us.

To exercise a right, email support@guanyisearch.com with the subject line “Privacy Request”. We may ask for what is necessary to verify the request and will respond as applicable law requires.

## 9. California notice

For California residents, certain uses of IP addresses, device identifiers and related technical information may be considered “sharing” under California privacy law. You may ask us to opt you out of applicable sharing by emailing support@guanyisearch.com with the subject line “California Privacy Request”.

guanyisearch does not knowingly sell or share the personal information of California residents under 16 years of age.

## 10. Cookies and similar technology

Necessary browser storage and security-verification technology protect registration, maintain authenticated access and keep the service working; they cannot be disabled without affecting core features.

Your cookie preference is recorded in your browser. Optional analytics and personalised advertising remain off until you choose them. When we add an optional measurement or advertising service, we enable it only after the matching preference has been selected, and update this policy before using it.

## 11. Security and contact

We use encryption in transit, access controls and other technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

For questions about this policy, contact support@guanyisearch.com.
