# Survey product foundation

## Product position

GuanyiSearch is a research service and participant panel. The public site sells the value of custom questionnaires and tailored studies; it does not describe internal qualification, routing, acceptance, supplier, or fulfilment decisions.

Public calls to action should use concise customer language such as `Discuss a project`, `Start a questionnaire`, and `Plan research`. Do not present a catalogue of audience attributes, automated fieldwork, instant results, pricing, or delivery mechanisms until each can be operated reliably.

## The minimum questionnaire lifecycle

The reference video is useful because it makes the customer workflow legible. The foundation should be implemented in this order:

1. **Design** — A confirmed client can name a questionnaire and add, edit, reorder, and remove questions. Initial supported question types: single choice, multiple choice, rating scale, short text, and long text.
2. **Preview** — The questionnaire can be tested in a respondent view before it is made available. Preview responses are never stored as live responses.
3. **Send** — A confirmed questionnaire can be made available to an approved set of panel participants, with a response limit and dates. This must remain unavailable until its targeting, consent, reward, and operational controls exist.
4. **Results** — The owner can see response counts, completion state, individual responses where permitted, and basic question summaries. Export and advanced analytics come later.

## Current truth

- The platform can collect research enquiries and save confirmed-client project requests.
- The panel can show internally approved research opportunities to participants and record applications.
- The platform does **not** yet support client-authored question sets, respondent-facing client questionnaire links, automated participant selection, live questionnaire distribution, or response analysis.

## Delivery rule

Do not expose a lifecycle stage in a customer workspace until the preceding stage has a persistent data model, permission boundary, validation, and a usable end-to-end path. A polished page alone is not a product capability.
